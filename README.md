# My Only Favorite RomCom Movie (50 First Dates)
## (As someone who doesn't sit through a whole movie)

  Growing up, I came to dislike watching through a movie's whole duration. Probably because I spent my childhood watching them on the TV, and now I have my own room with no TV in it.
I do like to watch the more mainstream ones. Marvel, some Disney, Video Game to Movie Adaptations, and other films catered for the niche audience.

However, there is this one movie that I tend to rewatch whenever I feel like it. Add to the fact that I never really rewatch films after I've enjoyed them.

This movie is titled 50 First Dates. It is a romcom film starring Adam Sandler and Drew Barrymore that is about a girl (Drew) losing her memories everyday and the guy (Adam) pursuing her every single day with a new approach.

It's a movie with a unique take on amnesia and how love managed to overcome it. I personally think the conclusion is realistically impossible but I do enjoy watching through it.

The reason I liked the movie so much is because it was probably the first "live action" movie that I really understood when I was young. I graduated from animated movies by watching this film.

I still remember the first time I watched it. Not from our home, not from the phone, not from a computer. It was from the TV on a bus while we were on our way home from a fiesta. I couldn't really sleep on the bus, so I watched it. The movie was simple enough for a seven year old kid to understand it.

I love its comedy, ranging from simple harmless jokes to mature jokes that I get to understand once I rewatched it as a teenager.

I love the main couple's dynamic. Adam and Drew actually had more movies together, but 50 First Dates remains as my top 1.

And the reason why I hold this movie so dearly is that it was actually released on the day of my birthday. It was already my favorite before I did some research, and it was like fate giving me a gift and said "hey you'll grow up to love this."

That is why 50 First Dates is my favorite film in the romance-comedy genre. I have a lot of favorites, but nothing changes my opinion on this one.
